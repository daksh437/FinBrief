## Full description (4000 chars max)

```
FinBrief turns Indian financial news into something you can actually read in a
minute — and tells you which of it matters to the stocks you hold.

WHY ANOTHER NEWS APP?

Most market news is written for people who already understand markets. FinBrief
takes the same stories and explains them: what happened, what it concerns, and
why it's in the news — in plain English or Hindi, in about 30 seconds of
reading.

NEWS THAT KNOWS WHAT YOU HOLD

Add your stocks to your watchlist or portfolio, and FinBrief filters the day's
news down to what touches them. The clever part is that it doesn't just match
company names: an RBI policy story never mentions "HDFC Bank", but if you hold
it, that story reaches you anyway.

WHAT'S INSIDE

• Live tape — a fast, scrolling feed of market headlines as they land, from
  Economic Times, Business Standard, LiveMint, Moneycontrol and more
• AI summaries — the key points of any story, in seconds
• Hindi translation — read any story in simple, conversational Hindi
• Listen — have a story read aloud, hands-free, using your phone's own voice
• What this affects — which parts of the market a story concerns
• In Focus Today — the companies today's news is actually about, with live
  prices
• Market data — Sensex, Nifty, gold, USD/INR, crypto and global stocks
• Ask AI — ask what a term means or why something happened, in your own words
• Breaking alerts — plus a morning and an evening brief
• Bookmarks, reading history, and search across everything

FREE AND PREMIUM

Free: all the news, the live tape, market data, and 5 AI actions every day.
Premium: unlimited AI, no ads, all alerts, and unlimited portfolio holdings.

New subscribers get their first month for ₹49, then ₹199/month. 6-month and
yearly plans are cheaper per month. Cancel anytime in Google Play.

IMPORTANT

FinBrief is a news app. It is not investment advice, and it is not a
recommendation to buy, sell or hold anything. It does not rate securities,
predict prices, or set targets. We are not a SEBI-registered investment adviser
or research analyst — please speak to one before making any investment
decision.

Summaries are generated by AI and can be wrong. Every story links to the
original article, and we'd encourage you to read it.

Market prices come from third-party sources, may be delayed, and are not
suitable for trading decisions.

Privacy Policy: https://finbrief-backend.onrender.com/privacy
Terms: https://finbrief-backend.onrender.com/terms
Delete your account: https://finbrief-backend.onrender.com/delete-account
```

*~2,100 characters. The disclaimer block is deliberately in the listing, not
just in-app: a reviewer looking at a finance app checks for exactly this, and
users who read it before installing complain less afterwards.*

### App access — REQUIRED, and a common rejection

FinBrief needs a login, so a reviewer cannot see anything without an account.
Play rejects apps that don't provide credentials. Under **App access**, choose
"All or some functionality is restricted" and supply a working demo account:

```
Email:    demo@finbrief.app
Password: (set one and record it here)
Instructions: Sign in with the details above. All features are visible on the
free tier. AI actions are limited to 5 per day.
```

Create that account in the app before submitting, and don't delete it.

### Financial features declaration

Play asks whether the app provides financial services. FinBrief does **not** —
no trading, lending, payments, or investment advice. It is a news aggregator
with AI summaries. Answer accordingly.
